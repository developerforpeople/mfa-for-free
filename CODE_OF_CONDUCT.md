# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

NLR Identity is a place to learn how authentication works. We want it to be usable by people who
are encountering this material for the first time, which means keeping it free of harassment and
condescension.

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Behaviour that builds this community:

- Answering "beginner" questions as if they were good questions, because they are
- Giving feedback on code rather than on the person who wrote it
- Accepting correction gracefully, and offering it kindly
- Assuming good faith when a message reads badly

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Dismissing or mocking someone for not knowing something
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at `conduct@nlr-identity.dev`
*(replace with the maintainer address for your fork)*. All reports will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they
consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Maintainers who do not follow or
enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face consequences decided by other project leaders.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and code
review — and when an individual is officially representing the project in public.
